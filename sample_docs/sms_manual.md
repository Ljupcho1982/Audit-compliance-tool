# SMS Manual - Skyline Regional Airways

## 1. Hazard identification
Hazards are identified through the voluntary and mandatory occurrence reporting system, flight data monitoring, ramp inspections, and internal audits. All reports are entered in the safety database within 72 hours by the Safety Office.

## 2. Risk assessment
Each hazard is rated using the 5x5 risk matrix for probability (1-5) and severity (A-E). Risks rated in the red zone are intolerable and operations must stop until controls are in place. Yellow-zone risks require mitigation approved by the Safety Manager. Mitigations are recorded in the hazard register.

## 3. Safety performance monitoring
The Safety Review Board tracks safety performance indicators every quarter, including the voluntary report rate, unstable approach rate and ground damage events. Internal audits cover each department once per year and findings are tracked to closure.

## 4. Emergency response
The Emergency Response Plan (document ERP-01) defines the roles of the crisis team, the contact tree, and coordination with airport authorities and local emergency services. The plan is exercised once every two years.

## 5. Training
New employees receive a one-hour safety induction. Flight crew complete annual recurrent safety training.
