# Skyline Regional Airways - Safety Policy

Skyline Regional Airways is committed to the safety of its passengers, crew and the public.

The Chief Executive Officer, Maria Kovac, is the Accountable Executive and holds ultimate responsibility for the Safety Management System. She approves all safety objectives and ensures the resources needed to meet them are available.

Safety objectives for 2026:
- Reduce reportable runway excursions to zero.
- Achieve a voluntary safety report rate of at least 5 reports per 100 flight hours.
- Complete 100% of internal safety audits on schedule.

The Director of Safety, Daniel Petrov, is appointed as Safety Manager. He is responsible for operating the SMS, maintaining the hazard register, and reporting directly to the Accountable Executive.

All managers are responsible for safety within their departments. All employees are required to report hazards and occurrences without fear of punishment, except in cases of wilful misconduct.

Signed: Maria Kovac, CEO, 12 January 2026
