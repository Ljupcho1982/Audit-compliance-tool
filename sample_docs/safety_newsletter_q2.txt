Skyline Safety Matters - Issue 7

Our voluntary reporting rate this quarter was 3.1 reports per 100 flight hours, below our target of 5. Please keep reporting.

Lesson learned: A ground handling vehicle was parked within the wingtip clearance zone at Gate 4. Following the report, marked clearance lines were repainted and the ramp briefing was updated.

Safety briefings are held monthly in the crew room. Minutes are posted on the staff noticeboard.
