## Hazard identification (binding requirement, paraphrase)
The operator shall implement a process to identify aviation safety hazards that result from its activities, evaluate them, and manage the associated risks.

## Hazard identification (guidance, paraphrase)
Both reactive and proactive schemes should be the formal means of collecting, recording, analysing and acting on hazards. A reactive scheme alone, such as occurrence reporting, is not sufficient; a proactive scheme such as risk-based audits or safety surveys should operate beside it.

## Contracted activities (binding requirement, paraphrase)
When the operator contracts or purchases services or products, it shall ensure that those services or products comply with the applicable requirements.
